Engine Lab principle: measurement is objective; scoring is policy. Each attempt preserves raw pitch, stabilized pitch, confidence, timing and voiced coverage. Changing Relaxed → Virtuoso changes scoring tolerances, not the underlying measurements.

For focus-group testing, use the same few phrases across singers, hummers and whistlers. After each attempt, rate whether the score felt fair, then copy the validation data for comparison.
